  • Indices (SP500, NQ100)
  • U.S. Stocks (NASDAQ, NYSE)

🌐 3.3 Leverage — All Countries

The same leverage rules apply to all countries. Leverage depends on the instrument, not on the client's equity.

📋
What changed
  • Floating leverage based on equity has been removed
  • No leverage reduction during news releases or around market open/close
  • The separate leverage configuration for Uzbekistan & Kyrgyzstan (UZ & KGZ) has been removed — one set of rules for every country
🔄
Existing accounts

Existing accounts are moved to the new fee group once all open positions are closed. Clients may also open a new account to switch right away (see Module 2, section 2.4).
