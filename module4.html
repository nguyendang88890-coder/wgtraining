🛡️ 4.4 IB Reward Eligibility & Anti-Fraud Policy

⏱️
Minimum Holding Time Requirement

IB Rewards are only recorded for positions with an open-to-close duration exceeding 120 seconds.

🚫
Violations / Invalid Trading Activities

In cases of cheating, price manipulation, or latency arbitrage:

  • The violating account will have all profits confiscated
  • The IB will have all IB Rewards generated from the violating trades deducted

🏆 4.5 IB Power Rewards (Monthly Bonus)

Introducing Brokers who meet the qualifying criteria within a calendar month are eligible to receive a bonus of up to USD 1,000.

🆕
What changed
  • The Net Deposit threshold and the required number of new clients are reduced for all tiers
  • Trading volume requirements and bonus amounts are unchanged
  • The number of new clients is now a minimum threshold (2+, 3+, …) instead of a range
  • Effective immediately — existing IBs are evaluated on the new criteria
✅
Qualified New Funded Client

A new client counts toward the tiers above only if all of the following are true:

  • Makes their first deposit with WeGolden within the month
  • Completes KYC before the monthly cut-off date
  • Does not share a device, payment method or bank account with the IB or with other clients
  • First deposit of at least USD 150
  • Makes no withdrawal of principal capital during the month (withdrawing profits is permitted)
  • Trades a minimum of 0.5 lots round-turn, on at least 2 different trading days
  • No hedging or coordinated trading
  • Has no trades closed in under 25 seconds
📌
Notes
  • Rewards are calculated on a monthly basis
  • All 3 criteria of a tier (Net Deposit, Volume, Qualified New Funded Clients) must be met within the same month
  • IBs receive only the highest tier reached each calendar month
  • The reward is credited as commission within 7 business days after the month ends
  • Not applicable to WeGolden employees or net revenue share partners
  • Net deposits are calculated as total deposits minus withdrawals
  • Trading volume is calculated in round-turn lots on eligible accounts
  • Matching IP addresses are sent to a compliance review — they no longer cause automatic disqualification

📢 4.6 IB Reward Update — New Conditions

To be eligible to withdraw the IB reward, each IB must meet the following requirements:

🚫
  • Each IB must refer at least 2 new clients
  • Each referred client must:
    • Complete full verification
    • Deposit a minimum of USD 500
    • Trade at least 1 standard lot
    • Be a new client — excluding those moved or transferred from other IBs
📌

Please notice and inform the customer of these conditions before they subscribe to the IB program.

🎁 4.7 Welcome IB Bonus (One-time USD 3,000)

A one-time bonus of USD 3,000 for new IBs who meet all of the conditions below during their qualifying period. Each IB ID can receive it only once. (Shown as "Welcome IB Reward" on wegolden.com.)

EvaluationDaily
Qualifying PeriodA fixed 6-month window, starting from the date the IB's registration is approved
ConditionsAll three must be satisfied at the same time (see below)
🎯
All three conditions
  • IB Tree Net Deposit ≥ USD 88,000 (cumulative) — total deposits − withdrawals − rewards already paid, across the IB's direct referrals
  • 2 consecutive months with a positive Net Deposit
  • ≥ 20 Qualified Clients — direct referrals only
✅
Qualified Client — all four criteria
  • KYC Level 1 completed (ID + Address + Selfie)
  • Net Deposit ≥ USD 150
  • Traded ≥ 0.5 Standard Lot Equivalent — only closed positions with a movement of at least 3 pips count
  • Account Move Rule — no prior deposit under a previous IB code
📢

These terms are effective immediately. Existing IBs are evaluated on the new criteria for both IB Power Rewards and the Welcome IB Bonus.

⏳
Holding period & payment
  • The bonus is held for 60 days after the conditions are met
  • It is not paid if the IB Tree Net Deposit drops by more than 50%, if fraud is detected, or if the IB agreement is terminated
  • After the holding period it is credited to the Wallet within 7 business days and can be withdrawn in full
