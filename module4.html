IB Policies & Reward

How Introducing Brokers earn rewards, commission structure, and monthly bonuses

🤝 4.1 Standard Account

High Spread (HS) — suffix "i", e.g. XAUUSDi

Fees/CommissionCommission-free
IB Reward (Forex & Gold)USD 16 / lot
SpreadCompetitive floating spreads — from 19 points (USDCHF), from 33 points (Gold)
Revenue Sharing RatioUp to 70% of revenue (depending on configuration/agreement)

Low Spread (LS) — suffix "m", e.g. XAUUSDm (Default account)

Fees/CommissionCommission-free
IB Reward (Forex & Gold)USD 8 / lot
SpreadCompetitive floating spreads — from 12 points (EURUSD), from 22 points (Gold)
Revenue Sharing RatioUp to 70% of revenue (depending on configuration/agreement)

⚡ 4.2 ECN Account

High Commission (HC) — suffix "e", e.g. XAUUSDe

Fees/CommissionUSD 20/lot (major Forex & metals) · USD 22/lot (cross Forex pairs) — charged on position open
IB Reward70% of commission ≈ USD 16 / lot
SpreadCompetitive raw spreads — EURUSDe from 1–3 points, XAUUSDe from 8 points

Low Commission (LC) — suffix "e", e.g. XAUUSDe (Default account)

Fees/CommissionUSD 5/lot, charged on position open
IB Reward70% of commission ≈ USD 3.5 / lot
SpreadCompetitive raw spreads — EURUSDe from 1–3 points, XAUUSDe from 8 points

👥 4.3 Master & Investor Accounts — Copy Trading

Master Account (MA) / Investor Account (IA) — suffix "u", e.g. XAUUSDu. Target users: Master Traders & Investors (Copiers) using WeGolden Copy Trading.

Fees/CommissionCommission-free
IB Reward (Forex & Gold)USD 16 / lot
SpreadFloating spreads from 22 points (EURUSD), 32 points (Gold)
Revenue Sharing RatioUp to 70% of revenue (depending on configuration/agreement)

Multi-Level Commission Policy

A Master IB receives:

  • 10% of income generated by direct Sub-IBs
  • 1% of income generated by direct Sub-Sub-IBs

🛡️ 4.4 IB Reward Eligibility & Anti-Fraud Policy

⏱️
Minimum Holding Time Requirement

IB Rewards are only recorded for positions with an open-to-close duration exceeding 120 seconds.

🚫
Violations / Invalid Trading Activities

In cases of cheating, price manipulation, or latency arbitrage:

  • The violating account will have all profits confiscated
  • The IB will have all IB Rewards generated from the violating trades deducted
