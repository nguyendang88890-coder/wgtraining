Deposit & Withdrawal

How clients fund and withdraw from their trading accounts

💳 5.1 Deposit & Withdrawal

  • Fast and secure deposits & withdrawals via USDT or Bank Transfer (QR Code)
  • Multiple payment methods available in most countries
  • Users can prioritize the USDT gateway for faster transactions

📋 5.2 Transaction Rules

ℹ️
  • USDT: Withdrawals must be made to the same wallet address used for deposits
  • Bank: Deposits and withdrawals must use a bank account under the same account holder's name
  • All transactions comply with identity verification (KYC) requirements

🔐 5.3 Processing & Security

✅
  • Withdrawals are completed within 5 minutes
  • Funds are sent directly to the user's registered bank account or USDT wallet
  • Protected by multi-layer authentication, data encryption, and 24/7 transaction monitoring
  • Transaction history and status updates are available for full transparency

⚠️ 5.4 Fee Notes

📌
  • WeGolden covers all deposit and withdrawal gateway fees
  • No fees are charged to normal clients
  • WeGolden may apply fees to accounts with little/no trading activity, or suspected abuse through excessive deposits and withdrawals, in accordance with company regulations
